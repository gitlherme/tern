Features

The switcher your Mac is missing.

⌥⇥

Windows, not apps

Every window is a card with a preview, in the order you used them. The previous one is always a tap away.

⌫

Hide what gets in the way

Hide a whole app or just one window. Tern stops showing them until you take them off the list.

⌘

Your shortcut

Record any combination. Tern intercepts it so it doesn’t also fire in the frontmost app.

◐

Out of the way

Lives in the menu bar, with no Dock icon. Native Swift, lightweight, no account, no tracking.

Shortcuts

All from the keyboard.

  • Open and move forward ⌥ ⇥
  • Move back ⇧ ⇥
  • Focus the window ⏎
  • Hide the app ⌫
  • Hide just the window ⌥ ⌫
  • Close the switcher esc
