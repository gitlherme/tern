Your turn, right window.

Tern is a window switcher for Mac that lives in your menu bar. Press ⌥ ⇥ to switch, type to find the window, and hide the apps that just get in the way.

Features

The switcher your Mac is missing.

⌥⇥ Windows, not apps

Every window is a card with a preview, in the order you used them. The previous one is always a tap away. Without Screen Recording, the card shows the icon and Tern still switches.

abc Type to find it

With the switcher open, start typing. “vsc” finds Visual Studio Code by the app name or the window title.

⌘W Act without leaving

⌘ W closes the highlighted window, ⌘ M minimizes it, and ⌘ Q quits the app. All from the switcher.

◐ Out of the way

Lives in the menu bar, with no Dock icon. Native Swift, lightweight, no account, no tracking. Record a different shortcut in Settings.

Out of the switcher.

  • The whole app ⌫ keeps it out until you bring it back, in Settings or with a link.
  • Just one window ⌥ ⌫ hides it by its exact title. The rest of the app still shows up.
  • By title A piece of text, or a * pattern, hides windows like Picture in Picture even when the name changes.
  • For a while ⇧ ⌫ hides an app for an hour. In Settings, until tomorrow too.
  • By mode Work, Personal: an extra set of apps. Switch it from the menu bar, or tie the mode to a macOS Focus.

Shortcuts

All from the keyboard.

  • Open and move forward ⌥ ⇥
  • Move back ⇧ ⇥
  • Focus the window ⏎
  • Search type
  • Hide the app ⌫
  • Snooze for 1 hour ⇧ ⌫
  • Hide just the window ⌥ ⌫
  • Close the window ⌘ W
  • Minimize ⌘ M
  • Quit the app ⌘ Q
  • Close the switcher esc

Arrow keys move the selection too. While searching, ⌫ deletes text and esc clears the search: letting go of ⌥ confirms only when you haven’t typed. ⌥ ⇥ is the default; record another shortcut in Settings. Tern intercepts it so it doesn’t also fire in the frontmost app.

Open it from anywhere.

In the Shortcuts app: open the switcher, hide an app, show it again, or change the mode. The same works from a tern:// link. Those links don’t close windows or quit apps.

  • tern://open opens the switcher
  • tern://hide?app=com.spotify.client&for=1h hides Spotify for an hour
  • tern://unhide?app=com.spotify.client shows it again
  • tern://mode?name=Work turns a mode on; without the name, turns it off

Switch windows without thinking.

Download Tern, grant Accessibility, and press ⌥ ⇥. If you already have it, the app installs 1.2 on its own.
